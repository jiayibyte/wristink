Alpine Privacy Notice
Developer: cherryeveli
Contact: cherryeveli@gmail.com

Alpine reads time, system state, selected activity/heart-rate/elevation/stress records, notifications, cached weather and an existing location record locally on the watch for display and approximate sky calculations.
It does not start GPS or continuously subscribe to sensors. It has no developer server, account sign-in, advertising, analytics SDK or custom network request. The developer does not receive, upload, sell or share health, activity or location data. Garmin weather and synchronization are subject to Garmin policies.
Only preferences are saved across restarts. Runtime caches stay on the watch. The store program does not emit health/location logs. Screenshots use labelled demo data; diagnostic/test programs are excluded from the installation package.
If you contact support, the developer receives only what you choose to send. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary health/location details.

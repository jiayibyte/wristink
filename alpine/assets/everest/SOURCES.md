# Everest artwork references

Original vector illustration and native-resolution rasterization in `tools/everest_art.py`.
It is a stylized, compressed composition, not a surveyed panorama or a photograph.
No external photograph is distributed in the watch-face resources.

Shape reference, inspected 2026-11-03:
- Colin Ingram, Everest north face from Rongbuk: https://www.caingram.com/Nepalpeaks/everest_north_side.htm
- Reference photograph: https://www.caingram.com/Everest_pix/Everest_north_face.jpg
- NASA, North Col of Mount Everest: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/north-col-of-mount-everest-48603/

Key visual structure: broad north face, small summit pyramid, stepped northeast skyline,
western shoulder, broad geometric snow planes, a glacier valley and foreground ridges.
Clouds, colour palettes, decorative sky effects and foreground composition are original.
The final monochrome composition is separately hand-drawn in solid black/white.
Dense texture/dither and summit spindrift were removed following user feedback.

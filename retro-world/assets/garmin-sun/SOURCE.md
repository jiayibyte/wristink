# Garmin sunrise / sunset reference

Official symbol: https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=agZJiZRjhX2adgWkBVOHI9
Article: What Do the Icons on My Garmin Watch Face Mean?
Label: Sunrise and Sunset. Retrieved 2026-10-28.

The original PNG is retained unchanged; source.json records its URL and SHA256.
The solid half-sun, three rays and horizontal horizon segments are traced on a
13x8 native pixel grid at build time, retaining one-pixel gaps between the six
separate ink components and included in the static dial. This adapts the official
published shape; it is not a pixel-exact extraction from Instinct firmware.

Layout checked against stock Instinct photos showing sunrise time, a shared sun
in the center, and sunset time. Public photo pages are listed in source.json.
Those third-party photographs are reference only and are not redistributed.

Rights in the original symbol remain with Garmin. No open-source license for
Garmin artwork is asserted.

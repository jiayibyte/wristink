Retro World · Map Watch Face

A compact instrument-style face with a world map, bold retro time, and everyday watch data.

• Original seven-segment digits and an analog sub-dial, with 12/24-hour time.
• 16 world-map skins selected from local weather, daylight, dawn and dusk. The global day/night shading moves gradually with time.
• A small marker shows an approximate location from an existing fix or fresh system-weather location. The face does not start GPS.
• Heart rate, steps, calories, elevation, daily distance, and the watch's estimated remaining battery days.
• Weather-dependent icons, day/night variants for clear and partly cloudy conditions, plus sunrise and sunset times.
• Classic monochrome on MIP displays. Warm ivory panels, a teal map and amber accents on AMOLED displays.
• Digital seconds and the second hand update only while the face is awake. AMOLED always-on mode uses dim outline time, minute-based movement and a rotating pixel-rest mask.

Data and compatibility
Use current watch firmware and keep Garmin Connect synchronized. Available data varies by device. Missing values show “--”; the live app never substitutes demonstration data. Battery days use the system estimate and display “--D” when unavailable. Units are Celsius, kilometres and metres. The watch face retains its retro English abbreviations; store information is available in English and Simplified Chinese.

The low-resolution world map and location marker are illustrative, not navigation tools. Weather textures reflect local conditions, not global weather observations.

Privacy and power
No developer server, advertising, analytics SDK, or custom weather requests. No continuous GPS or additional sensor subscription. Map and environment data are cached. Always-on mode does not update weather, the map or health metrics. Battery use varies with the watch, display settings and usage.

This is a free, independently developed watch face, not an official Garmin product. Store images contain simulated example data. The store's Compatible Devices list is the definitive device list.
